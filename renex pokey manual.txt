

    RENEX POKEY
    ===========
    v1.0
    18 Aug 2026
    
    
  A modern, high quality reimplementation of a POKEY-style sound engine.
    
  Designed for use with Game Maker 8.2.
    
    
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Pokey?
  ------  
  
  
  POKEY is a co-processor present in some of Atari's first home consoles. It
  was responsible for reading controller input and, among other things, had
  limited sound output capabilities through 4 builtin programmable sound
  generators using simplistic pulse and noise models. When you think of
  "dat Atari sound doe", that's a POKEY chip.
  
  This extension provides a modern reimplementation of the sound core of the
  POKEY chip, with customizable channel counts, high quality audio output,
  and optional accurate frequency response and stereo panning values. You can
  still completely ignore everything though, if you're going for accurate
  retro sound, you can use settings that match your expected sound aesthetic.
  
  
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Function list
  -------------
  
  
  pokey_init(samplerate,channels)
    Starts the sound engine with the desired settings.
    - 'samplerate' is limited between 8000 and 48000.
    - 'channels' is limited between 1 and 32.
    - pass 'noone' for 'samplerate' to use the system sample rate, which
      gives the highest quality audio.
  
  pokey_get_chromatic_frequency(note)
  pokey_get_byte_frequency(byte)
  pokey_sound(channel,type,freq,vol,pan)
  pokey_stop()
